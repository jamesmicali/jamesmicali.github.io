About

I partner with operations and executive leadership to turn financial insight into operating performance. As Senior Manager of Manufacturing & Operations Finance at Twist Bioscience, I own the P&L for a $180M operating budget spanning manufacturing, supply chain, quality, and biopharma — and I rebuilt the forecasting and CapEx processes behind it. Before that, I spent nine years at Intel across supply chain finance, program management, and engineering, leading teams and programs that generated nine figures of cost improvement.

My work sits at the intersection of finance and operations: gross margin forecasting the board relies on, sourcing strategies that move product cost, and planning processes that make capital allocation a strategic exercise instead of a spreadsheet one.
